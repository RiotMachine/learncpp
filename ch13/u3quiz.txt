Emumerators can be

Given an integer value? T
Given no explicit value? T
Given a floating point value? F
Given a negative value? T
Given a non-unique value? T
Given the value of a prior enumerator (e.g. magenta = red)? T
Given a non-constexpr value? F; enumerators are compile-time constants
	"since enumerators are constexpr, their values must be constexpr"
	(enumerator is the word, enumeration is the object)
